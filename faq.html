Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Brokex Protocol and how to get started.

What is Brokex?

Brokex is a decentralized CFD brokerage protocol that allows users to trade global markets without giving up custody of their funds.

How do I get started?

Connect your wallet, deposit your collateral, and you can start trading instantly. No KYC required for base levels.
